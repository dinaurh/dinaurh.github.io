I am a PhD student in quantitative ecology at the Centre for Research into Ecological and Environmental Modelling and Scottish Oceans Institute since 2024. My research sits at the intersection of bioacoustics, marine mammal science, statistics and computing.

My PhD focuses on quantitative methods to estimate harbour porpoise density from automated click detector data, but I have worked with a wide range of data, including but not limited to, animal-borne tag movement and acoustic data, broadband acoustic data, drone data and opportunistic and systematic visual survey data.
